About ExcelModels.in

Practical Excel tools for real-world finance work.

ExcelModels.in is designed to make useful financial and business templates easier to find, understand and use. The goal is simple: spend less time building spreadsheets from scratch and more time analysing the numbers.

What we focus on

  • Practical finance and FP&A templates
  • Clear, editable Excel models
  • Simple explanations and examples
  • Tools useful for professionals, students and business owners
